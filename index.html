Tower Rush Defense

Place towers. Crush waves. Survive the bosses.

How to Play

  • Click a tower in the shop, then click any non-path tile to place it.
  • Towers automatically shoot enemies that enter their range.
  • Earn money by defeating enemies; stronger enemies pay more.
  • Click a placed tower to see stats, upgrade it, or sell it.
  • Every 10th wave spawns a boss. Survive as long as you can!
  • Use the speed controls to fast-forward slow sections.
