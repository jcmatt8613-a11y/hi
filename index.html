How to Play

  • Pick a tower from the shop, then click any non-path tile to place it.
  • Towers auto-target enemies in range. Right-click or Esc cancels placement.
  • Click a placed tower to view stats, upgrade up to Lv 5 (Paragon), sell, or change targeting (First/Last/Strong/Close).
  • Earn money by killing enemies and clearing waves. Stronger enemies pay more.
  • Aura towers buff nearby towers; Econ towers generate gold; Anti-Air is required for flying enemies.
  • Spend money on abilities (Fireball, Ice Nova, Gold Rush, Lightning Storm). Hotkeys Q W E R.
  • Place a Hero once per run. Heroes gain XP from kills and have an active ability (hotkey H).
  • Every 10th wave spawns a unique boss. Survive as long as you can!
  • Hotkeys: Space pause · 1/2/3 speed · U upgrade · S sell · T targeting
