About The Channel

Welcome to
The Zone

The Kris Zone is where classic rock, grunge, nu metal and 80s/90s/2000s alternative get ranked, roasted and relived. Over 527 videos and 1,500+ subscribers strong — and growing every week. No fluff. No filler. Just real Gen X opinions on the music that mattered.

Expect reaction videos like "Nirvana Reaction: The Best Grunge Band Isn't Who You Think," "Only ONE Survives" showdowns like Pantera vs Metallica, hot takes like "Rick Beato Is Wrong About the 90s," and deep dives into bands from Slayer to Soundgarden.

From "Rust in Peace Is So Good It Destroyed Megadeth's Career" to "The Beatles Are OVERRATED And These 5 Bands Prove It" — if you grew up on MTV, Headbangers Ball and rock radio, this channel was made for you. Hit subscribe and join the conversation.

Rock History

The Gen X
Rock Timeline

The moments that changed everything. Kris has covered them all — and has opinions about every single one.

1978
Van Halen Debut — The Album That Started It All

Eddie Van Halen rewrote the rulebook for guitar. Kris covered this in his deep dive: the album that launched an entire era of shred and showmanship.

1983
Metallica — Kill 'Em All

Thrash metal is born. Nobody was ready. The riffs hit like a freight train and everything changed.

1986
Master of Puppets drops

The album that many call the greatest metal record ever made. Kris has a whole video on why the Black Album couldn't top it.

1990
Megadeth — Rust in Peace

So good it destroyed Megadeth's career — they could never top it. Kris breaks down exactly why in one of his most-watched videos (549 views and counting).

1991
The Year Rock Exploded — Three Legendary Albums

Nevermind. Ten. The Black Album. All in the same year. 1991 remains untouchable. Kris has proven Rick Beato wrong about this era.

1994
Kurt Cobain Dies — The End of an Era

Grunge's brightest light goes out. The music world mourns and nothing sounds the same again.

1996
Pantera's The Great Southern Trendkill

The most brutally heavy record of the decade. Dimebag at his most unhinged. Kris pitted Pantera against Metallica in the heaviest debate in metal.

2000
Linkin Park — Hybrid Theory

Nu-metal meets hip-hop meets emo and becomes one of the best-selling rock albums ever. Kris covered the Chester Bennington vs Fred Durst showdown.

2004
Dimebag Darrell Murdered on Stage

A nightmare. The rock world unites in grief. Pantera's legacy is forever sealed in tragedy and greatness.
